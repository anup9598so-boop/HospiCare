About us

Caring for you, every step of the way

HospiCare was founded to make quality healthcare easier to access, understand and manage.

Our mission

Health records and specialist care, connected

Many patients struggle to keep track of reports, prescriptions and appointments across different hospitals. HospiCare brings them together. One secure record follows you, and every specialist you book with can see the information you choose to share.

  • Personal health record with reports, vitals and history
  • Appointments with specialists across three partner hospitals
  • Clear pricing and transparent consultation fees
  • Data protected with industry-standard encryption

Our journey

How HospiCare grew

2019

HospiCare began with one clinic and a simple idea: a single record for every patient.

2021

Launched online appointment booking, reducing waiting times for specialist consultations.

2023

Partnered with three hospitals to offer cardiology, neurology and paediatric care under one account.

2026

Serving more than 25,000 patients with a secure, personal health record for every member.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and the change applies from your next billing date.

Are my health records secure?

All records are encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you and the doctors you authorise can view them.

Do plans cover consultation fees?

Plans cover booking and record access. Individual consultation fees are shown on each doctor's profile.
